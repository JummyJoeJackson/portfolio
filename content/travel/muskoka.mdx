Notes and photos from my trip to Muskoka, from the quiet mornings to the starry nights.

---

## Morning

I started each day with a walk down to the dock. The water was calm and the air was fresh. It was a beautiful morning. A little swim to wake me up, and then breakfast.

---

## Afternoon

Chilling with friends by the water. Sun was out, music was playing, and the vibes were immaculate.

---

## Evening

Board games and drinks by the fire. Paired with a homecooked meal. A perfect way to end the day.
